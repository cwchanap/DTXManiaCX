## Architecture and scope guardrails for the implementation agent

**Reuse rather than rebuild:** `PerformanceStage`, `PerformanceUILayout`, `NotationStripLayout`, `DTXChartParser.ChannelToLaneMap` through an internal lookup, `DrumStaffMapping`, `SkillPanelDisplay`, `ScoreDisplay`, `ComboDisplay`, `JudgementTextPopup` + internal wording/color maps, `TextHelper.TruncateToWidth`, `SongScore.CalculateGameSkill`, `JudgementManager`, `HitTimingFeedback.ProjectDelta`, current timer/progress, texture/font infrastructure.

**Do not touch:** notation quantization/scroll rate/engraving, hit marker x=220 or key-mask x=0..195, input binding or parsing **behavior**, audio, saved preference/telemetry shape, score/gauge/skill formulas, or DTXManiaNX. A narrow **read-only channel→lane accessor** in the parser and internal **wording/color visibility** in the existing popup manager are allowed; do not introduce public mutable mappings. Do not add custom art/SFX or a new rendering framework.

**Decision locked for this PR:** no notehead tint/fade. A >200ms Miss happens after the notehead is hidden by the key mask. A clear popup/label response is feasible now; notehead lifetime/geometry deserves a **separate, later ticket** only if playtests demand it.

**Time-box rule:** use one `TabHudDisplay`-owned **`JudgementTextPopup`** slot; do not build a second transient animation system or modify legacy popup spawning/rendering. **Score and combo are font-only in Tab**—do not attempt sprite relocation. On Tab HUD resource failure log and skip its draw, not a third/minimal HUD mode; existing notation renderer failure still selects Lanes. Prioritize correct mapping, legibility and Lanes regression over decoration.
